Privacy Policy — Math Brain Arena

Developer: Math Brain Studio
Last updated: November 18, 2026

Math Brain Studio respects the privacy of people who use Math Brain Arena, an educational mathematics game. This policy describes the current version of the app and will be updated when its features or data practices change.

Advertising and accounts

The current version of Math Brain Arena does not display advertisements, require account registration or login, or provide an online leaderboard. Advertising may be introduced in a future version, in which case this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Information and device services

We do not ask players to submit names, email addresses, or other personal details to play the current game. Some technical information may be processed by Android, Google Play, or other services involved in delivering the app. We are reviewing the app's technical dependencies to confirm whether any additional diagnostics, analytics, or other data processing occurs.

How information is used and shared

The game is intended to provide mathematics practice and entertainment. We do not operate an in-app account or online leaderboard in the current version. Information processed independently by Google Play is subject to Google's own privacy terms. If future app features involve additional collection, sharing, or third-party services, we will update this notice and applicable Google Play disclosures.

Children and families

Math Brain Arena contains educational mathematics activities. Parents and guardians may contact us with privacy questions. The app's intended age groups will be declared in its Google Play listing.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the app changes. The latest version will be available at this page with an updated date.
